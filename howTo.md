**## 3ChainTest**

`cd ~/Documents/ec_lightboard/sketches/3ChainTest`

**### Compile & Upload**
`arduino-cli compile --upload --fqbn esp32:esp32:esp32 .`

**### Upload**
`arduino-cli upload -p /dev/ttyUSB0 --fqbn esp32:esp32:esp32 .`
